## Position of AetherPolicy

AetherPolicy is the **ninth project** of the Aether family (candidate).
It is **not** a replacement for AetherIdentity (the eighth project).

**AetherPolicy has not been accepted as an independent architectural
dimension.** The project is paused at Phase 4 (Independence Decision), and
the decision is (E) undetermined.

See `ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESIS.md`, `FALSIFICATION/FALSIFICATION_PLAN.md`,
and `DECISIONS/INDEPENDENCE_DECISION.md`.

## How to contribute

This project is at Phase 4 (interim undetermined). Contributions at this
stage take the form of:

- Review of the hypothesis (`ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESIS.md`).
- Review of the responsibility boundaries (`research/RESPONSIBILITY_BOUNDARIES.md`).
- Review of the falsification conditions (`FALSIFICATION/FALSIFICATION_PLAN.md`, P-F1 to P-F5).
- Literature pointers on policy languages (XACML, Rego, Cedar), access control
  (RBAC, ABAC, ReBAC), policy lifecycle, and decentralized enforcement.
- Suggestions for the independent review (Phase 2, `REVIEW/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`).

See `CONTRIBUTING.md` for details.
