───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CENTRAL QUESTION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  How can policies be transformed from declarative rules into evaluable,
  enforceable, and revocable operational constraints across decentralized
  autonomous agent systems?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FIVE FUNCTIONS (INITIAL SCOPE)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Fn1 — Policy Definition      How is a policy expressed as a rule that
                              can be evaluated?
  Fn2 — Policy Evaluation      How is a policy determined to apply to an
                              action and its context?
  Fn3 — Policy Enforcement     How is a disallowed action prevented or
                              restricted?
  Fn4 — Policy Revocation      How is an authorization withdrawn during
                              operation?
  Fn5 — Policy Conflict        How are conflicting policies between agents
       Resolution             or environments resolved?

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ARCHITECTURAL HYPOTHESIS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  A potentially independent architectural gap may exist between governance
  authority and runtime monitoring: the operational management of policies
  as evaluable, enforceable, and revocable constraints across decentralized
  autonomous agents.

  This hypothesis is falsifiable.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  FALSIFICATION CONDITIONS (P-F1 TO P-F5)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  P-F1  Non-redundancy            Not falsified (with reservation)
  P-F2  Orthogonality             Not falsified (with reservation)
  P-F3  Boundary with governance  Not falsified (with reservation) -- MOST FRAGILE
  P-F4  Decentralizability        Not falsified (with reservation)
  P-F5  Observable benefit        Untested

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  INDEPENDENCE DECISION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Classification : (E) undetermined

  The argument is insufficient to accept AetherPolicy as an independent
  architectural dimension, and insufficient to reject it. A test is needed.

  The project is paused at Phase 4. Phase 5 (Specification) is not started.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES NOT CLAIM
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - That AetherPolicy is an independent architectural dimension.
  - That the five functions are complete.
  - That the boundaries with AetherGovern, AetherIdentity, and AetherRuntime
    are sufficient.
  - That the gap is genuine.
  - That implementation should begin.
